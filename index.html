Training After 50 Support

Welcome to the official support page for Training After 50. Training After 50 is a workout tracking and exercise reference app designed to help adults organize workouts, record their training, review previous performance, and access illustrated exercise guides.

Need Help?

If you have a question, encounter a problem, or would like to provide feedback about Training After 50, contact:

Email: thegreatstephens@aol.com

When reporting a problem, please include a brief description of what happened and, if possible, the screen or feature you were using.

Workout Data & Backups

Workout history is stored on your device. Training After 50 includes export and import backup features so you can create a backup of your workout history.

We recommend creating a backup periodically and before replacing your device, reinstalling the app, or making other significant changes to your device.

Privacy

Training After 50 does not require you to create an account or sign in. The app is designed to allow workout information to remain on your device unless you choose to export or share information yourself.

Important Information

Training After 50 provides fitness tracking and general exercise information. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult an appropriate healthcare professional before beginning or significantly changing an exercise program when appropriate for your circumstances.
